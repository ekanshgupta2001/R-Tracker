About This Tool

R-Tracker is a high-performance pathing visualizer designed to bridge the gap between code and the field. It allows teams to simulate autonomous paths and refine driver practice in a digital environment, saving hours of on-field calibration. This project was spearheaded by Ekansh Gupta and co-designed by Neel Laroiya of Team 25702 Rundle Robotics Castle.

Project Legacy

We believe that the best way to learn is to build. R-Tracker represents our commitment to high-level software engineering in FTC. By maintaining this tool, we provide a consistent standard for driver training and autonomous logic that future team members can build upon, fork, and improve.

Privacy & Data

R-Tracker is a set of static files. Nothing you type, score, or do is ever sent anywhere: there are no accounts, no database, no analytics, and no AI service.

  • Where your progress lives: only in this browser tab, and in a .json file you choose to export from the sidebar. Closing the tab clears it, so export before you leave.
  • What is in the file: driver practice scores and level results, curriculum progress, code submissions and theory answers, saved paths and strategies.
  • Sharing with a mentor: you send them your exported file yourself, the same way you would share any document. Nothing is uploaded by this site.
  • What the site remembers between visits: your light/dark theme choice, and nothing else.

Grading: theory answers and code submissions are checked by rules that run inside your browser. They never leave your device.
